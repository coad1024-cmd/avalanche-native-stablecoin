Direct inspection of `simulations/robustness_study/sobol_sensitivity.py` (lines 84–87) and `simulations/robustness_study/master_robustness_engine.py` (lines 146, 216–244) reveals the root cause:
- In `simulate_protocol_epoch`, the baseline price path has no shock on secondary AMM price $P_{\text{dex}}$; $P_{\text{dex}}$ moves only via unseeded Gaussian noise `np.random.normal(0.0, 0.001)`.
- Total variance $Var(Y)$ is dominated by unseeded random noise across runs.
- `sobol_sensitivity.py` enforces `S_i[i] = max(0.0, min(1.0, (np.mean(y_A * y_AB_i) - f_0_sq) / var_total))` and `S_Ti[i] = max(S_i[i], ...)`. When numerator / denominator evaluates to $\ge 1.0$, the clipping clamp hardcodes $S_i = 1.0000$ and $S_{Ti} \ge 1.0000$.
