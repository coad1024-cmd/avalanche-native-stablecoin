## Abstract

This proposal establishes the technical and economic specifications for **Avalanche Native USD (anUSD)**—an indigenous, capital-efficient, dual-class securitization stablecoin protocol for the Avalanche Primary Network (C-Chain) and Avalanche L1s (Subnets). 

Rather than relying on third-party centralized issuers or auction-based overcollateralization with liquidation contagion risk, anUSD implements an on-chain **Dual-Class Tranche Engine** with **Dynamic Upward ($H_u=2.0$) and Downward ($H_d=0.25$) State Resets** (based on SSRN-3856569). The protocol features mathematical immunity against instantaneous crashes up to $-60.0\%$, integrates $sAVAX$ liquid staking yield, and programmatically executes the **ACP-67 Yield Recycling Waterfall** (50–75% AVAX buyback & burn, 15–25% validator incentives, 15–25% ecosystem growth).

---

## Motivation

Stablecoin liquidity is the lifeblood of decentralized finance. While Avalanche hosts significant volumes of USDC and USDT, the network currently captures negligible economic value from these external assets. Furthermore:
1. **Capital Inefficiency**: Traditional CDP models lock up $150\%+$ collateral in idle vaults with auction delays that risk bad debt during flash crashes.
2. **Value Extraction**: Off-chain reserve yields generated by Avalanche users accrue exclusively to external private corporations.
3. **Cross-L1 Fragmentation**: Bridging stablecoins between Avalanche L1s requires wrapped synthetic representations subject to third-party bridge vulnerabilities.

anUSD solves these systemic challenges by establishing an Avalanche-native sovereign monetary standard that recycles all economic surplus directly into AVAX demand.

## Specification

### 1. Dual-Class Tranche Mechanism

The protocol accepts $AVAX$ and $sAVAX$ as vault collateral, splitting value into equal pairs of:
- **Class A (Senior Fixed-Income Token)**: Yields a continuous coupon rate $R = 7.3\%$ p.a.
- **Class B (Leveraged Long Token)**: Inherits $2.0\times$ initial leverage on AVAX price movements without ongoing funding fees or rollover liquidations.

Class A is further split 1:1 into:
- **Class A′ (anUSD Stablecoin)**: Dollar-pegged stablecoin ($1.00$) yielding benchmark money-market rate $R' \approx 3.0\%$ p.a.
- **Class B′ (Yield-Maximizer Token)**: Captures leveraged spread yield $(2R - R' = 11.6\%)$ p.a.

### 2. $O(1)$ Dynamic Reset State Machine

- **Upward Reset ($H_u = \$2.00$)**: Triggered when Class B NAV reaches $\$2.00$. Harvests excess gains to Class B in collateral, pays accrued Class A coupons, and executes a forward share split to reset NAVs to $\$1.00$ and restore $2.0\times$ leverage.
- **Downward Reset ($H_d = \$0.25$)**: Triggered when Class B NAV falls to $\$0.25$. Pays accrued coupons and amortized principal to Class A, executing an automated share merger (reverse split) to reset NAVs to $\$1.00$. Zero bad debt; zero liquidation auctions.

### 3. ACP-67 Yield Recycling Waterfall

All protocol revenue ($sAVAX$ staking rewards, mint/redeem fees, flash loan fees) is routed to the `YieldRecycler` contract:

| Bucket | Share | On-Chain Execution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **AVAX Buyback & Burn** | **65.0%** | Automated Uniswap V3 / Trader Joe swaps into AVAX followed by immediate native burning (`0x0000...dead` / native fee burn). |
| **Validator Staking Boost** | **20.0%** | Direct distribution to Avalanche P-Chain / C-Chain registered active validator addresses. |
| **Ecosystem & L1 Growth** | **15.0%** | Liquidity mining incentives for native DEX pairs and L1 Teleporter bridge rewards. |

## Security & Backwards Compatibility

1. **Model-Free Crash Bounds**: Analytical proofs establish that Class A′ stablecoins cannot incur principal loss unless the price of AVAX plunges $>60.0\%$ in a single oracle monitoring block.
2. **Reentrancy & MEV Protection**: Resets follow a strict two-phase commit-settlement flow to prevent flash-loan sandwich exploitation.
3. **Compatibility**: Fully compliant with ERC-20, ERC-4626, and Avalanche Teleporter (ICM) standards. Existing USDC/USDT assets remain completely unaffected.
