## 1. Families & Composition Rules

| Family | Operational Purpose | Structures (Catalog) |
|---|---|---|
| **Rhetorical** | Why the reader should care | Executive Summary · Motivation · Problem Statement |
| **Methodological** | How we know it | Securitization Model · Jump-Diffusion SDE · Dynamic Resets · PIDE Valuation · GDS State Transitions |
| **Epistemic** | What we now know, how firmly | Monte Carlo Trajectories · Crash Tolerance Proofs · Stability Heatmaps · KPI Scorecards |
| **Dialogic** | What decisions are required | Governance Levers · Barrier Threshold Decisions · Parameter Recommendations |
| **Cartographic** | Where this sits in the larger space | Avalanche ACP-67 Alignment · Cross-L1 Teleporter Routes · Threat Models · References |

---

## 2. Composition Rules
1. **Rhetorical Opening:** Every memo opens with a concise executive summary and motivation.
2. **Methodological Precedence:** No empirical or epistemic claim may appear without upstream methodological derivation.
3. **Exhibit Mandate:** Every section must carry exactly one complete exhibit (tensor table, house Mermaid diagram, or chart).
4. **Dialogic Closure:** Memos must conclude with concrete governance choices or parameter sign-offs.
